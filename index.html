How the calculation works

Coppell says the two 9-week marking-period grades together make up 90% of the semester grade, and the semester exam makes up 10%. GPA points are assigned to semester averages.

Important: Choose the exact level assigned to the course in the official Coppell course guide. Not every course described as advanced, honors, or dual credit automatically qualifies for the same level. Semester grades below 70 receive 0 GPA points.
